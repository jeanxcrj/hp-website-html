About the tour

At a time of rapid transformation in how we learn, design, engineer, and create, the HP USA College Tour brings together students, educators, technology partners, and industry leaders to explore the future of innovation. Through a unique combination of hands-on experiences and real-world insights, the tour showcases how emerging technologies are reshaping creative and technical workflows across industries.

Each event will feature a dynamic mix of workshops, industry keynotes, student showcases, technical demonstrations, and interactive activations, creating opportunities for students to engage directly with the technologies and professionals driving the future of work.

Alongside HP, leading technology and software partners including Intel, AMD, D5 Render, Bentley Systems, and SOLIDWORKS will showcase the latest advancements powering architecture, engineering, design, manufacturing, visualization, and AI-driven workflows. Students will gain firsthand exposure to cutting-edge hardware platforms, professional software tools, and industry best practices used by leading organizations around the world.

Visitors will have the opportunity to network directly with professionals from HP and participating partners. Whether exploring high-performance computing, real-time visualization, design simulation, digital twins, engineering workflows, or AI-assisted creation, attendees will gain valuable insight into the technologies shaping their future careers.

On the student side, each school is curating its own vision for how these new technologies are shaping the way we work. Whether architecture, engineering, design, or tech clubs, each stop along the tour will add a new series of works to the conversation.

Join us for a full day of inspiration, learning, networking, workshops, exhibitions, demonstrations, giveaways, and collaboration as we explore the future of creation together.

Featured Partners: HP, Intel, AMD, D5 Render, Bentley Systems, SOLIDWORKS, and participating university organizations.

Disclaimer: Participating speakers, technology partners, software vendors, exhibitors, workshops, and activities may vary by university and tour location.

Win a HP laptop

In addition to the demonstrations and exhibitions, the gallery space at each school will feature an interactive competition. Visitors will walk up to a large touchscreen display to tackle a quiz: Which Photo Has Been Altered with AI? At a time when AI-generated content is increasingly called into question, we are rewarding those with the best eye for spotting the difference.

Each question offers a choice between two options: an unaltered image and the same image slightly tweaked with AI. Choose the altered image and move on. Choose the unaltered image, and the quiz is over. A large LED display will showcase the leaderboard for the highest scorers.

Disclaimer: ties between students will be settled by a random pick. The exact number of laptop giveaways at each school is subject to availability.
