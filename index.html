01 / ABOUT

A year of build nights, on the road.

The HP College Tour brings the workstation floor to campus. Bring a project and an idea of where it is stuck; leave with it rendering on hardware you could not otherwise get your hands on.

Every stop runs the same shape. A morning of short talks from people who ship, an afternoon of open benches with engineers on the floor, and an evening where the room shows what it made that day.

It is free, and it is open to any enrolled student. No portfolio review, no application, no prior experience with any of the software on the benches.
